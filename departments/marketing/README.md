# Marketing

content, brand, distribution

23 jobs. Stage: launch.

## Insights
- `performance-mining` (solo): Rank our own content by what worked and what to redo.
- `trend-monitoring` (solo): Watch the niche for breaking topics and rising formats.
- `competitor-analysis` (solo): Collect and transcribe competitor content; extract the hook patterns.
- `audience-analysis` (solo): Mine comments and DMs for questions, objections and content ideas.

- `script-writing` (solo): Full short-form and long-form video scripts in the house voice.
- `caption-writing` (solo): Platform-correct captions with the CTA in the right place.
- `carousel-production` (solo): Design and export branded carousel slides.
- `video-production` (solo): Programmatic video: overlays, captions, motion graphics on recordings.
- `image-generation` (solo): Brand-consistent images from a reference library.
- `thumbnail-and-cover-design` (solo): Thumbnails and covers tuned to earn the click.
- `ad-creative` (assisted): Generate and iterate paid-ad variations: hooks, angles, formats, with the budget brief you approve.
- `landing-page-copy` (solo): Conversion copy for the whole page: hero, proof, offer, FAQ, CTA.
- `deck-production` (solo): Webinar and talk decks in the brand system.

## Repurposing

- `clip-extraction` (solo): Cut long recordings into short clips worth posting.
- `lead-magnet-builds` (solo): Turn content themes into gated guides and their landing pages.

## Distribution
