# Back Office

money in, books, office, people

18 jobs. Stage: run.

## Money In
- `invoice-generation` (solo): Branded invoices per entity, currency and tax treatment.
- `payment-tracking` (solo): What is paid, due and late, without opening a bank app.
- `collections` (assisted): Overdue invoices chased politely and persistently.

## Reporting
- `revenue-reporting` (solo): Monthly revenue truth across ventures and currencies.
- `goal-pacing` (solo): Tracking against the income target with pace forecasts.

## Finance Ops
- `cash-flow-forecasting` (assisted): Project cash in and out; know the runway before it is an emergency.
- `budget-tracking` (solo): Spend versus budget by category, ad spend first, overruns flagged early.

## Records
- `crm-sync` (solo): Deals, contacts and statuses stay current everywhere.
- `expense-categorisation` (solo): Spend categorised for the accountant before they ask.
- `document-filing-and-retrieval` (solo): Every document named, filed and findable in seconds.
- `contract-lifecycle` (assisted): Renewals, expiries and obligations tracked so nothing sneaks up.
- `entity-compliance` (human): Filings and obligations per entity, tracked and scheduled; you file.

## Office
- `calendar-management` (assisted): Scheduling, conflicts, buffers and timezone maths.
- `email-triage` (solo): The inbox sorted by what actually needs a human.

## Talent
- `candidate-sourcing` (solo): Find and rank candidates for open roles across platforms.
- `screening-and-scheduling` (human): First-pass screens and interview logistics; the decision is yours.
- `onboarding-and-training` (assisted): New people get playbooks, access and a thirty-day plan.
- `hr-and-policy-assistant` (assisted): Answer staff policy questions from the real handbook.
