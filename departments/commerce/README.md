# Commerce

sourcing, listings, fulfilment, marketplaces

Not in SkillTree. Added for shops, TikTok Shop and dropshipping ventures: physical or digital products sold online. Only runs when the venture kind is shop, tiktok-shop or dropshipping.

16 jobs. Stage: build.

## Sourcing
- `product-selection` (assisted): Shortlist products with demand, margin and low return risk from marketplace and social signals; Charlie picks.
- `supplier-research` (solo): Find and compare suppliers: price, minimums, lead time, location, reviews, UK delivery options.
- `sample-and-quality-check` (human): What to order as a sample and what to check when it arrives; the ordering and the verdict are Charlie's.
- `landed-cost-modelling` (solo): Unit cost plus shipping, duty, VAT, platform fees, payment fees and returns, into a margin per unit and a break-even ad cost.

## Listings
- `listing-optimisation` (solo): Titles, bullets, descriptions and keywords per marketplace, in the house voice, within each platform's rules.
- `product-photography-brief` (solo): Shot list and style notes for product images and short videos; what to shoot, in what order, for which placement.
- `pricing-and-offers` (assisted): Price points, bundles and launch offers that clear the margin floor; Charlie approves any price.
- `catalogue-sync` (solo): One product catalogue file feeding the store, the marketplace and the ad feed; stock and price stay consistent.

## Fulfilment
- `order-routing` (assisted): Each order goes to the right supplier or stock with the right details; failures flagged the same hour.
- `shipping-and-tracking` (solo): Tracking numbers collected and sent to the customer; late parcels chased before the customer asks.
- `returns-and-refunds` (assisted): Returns handled to UK consumer law and the platform's policy: approve, refund, replace or escalate, with the reason logged.
- `inventory-watch` (solo): Stock levels and supplier availability watched; listings paused before an order cannot be filled.

## Marketplace
- `tiktok-shop-setup` (assisted): The checklist and settings to open and configure a TikTok Shop or a store on another marketplace; Charlie signs up and pays nothing before revenue.
- `affiliate-and-creator-programme` (assisted): Commission rates, sample offers and outreach lists for creators who could sell the product; Charlie approves commissions.
- `marketplace-compliance` (solo): Product safety, labelling, restricted categories and marketplace policies checked before anything is listed.
- `review-management` (assisted): Reviews and ratings watched, replies drafted in the house voice, recurring complaints fed back to product selection.
