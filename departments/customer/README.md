# Customer

support, success, community

14 jobs. Stage: support.

## Support
- `ticket-triage` (solo): Classify, prioritise and route every support request as it lands.
- `faq-and-self-serve` (solo): Answer the questions that repeat and turn them into living help content.
- `escalations` (human): Spot the angry, the churn risk and the edge case, and get a human there.
- `macro-authoring` (solo): Turn repeating tickets into on-brand canned replies.

## Success
- `onboarding-journeys` (assisted): Guide every new user through activation step by step.
- `health-scoring` (solo): Score every account on usage, sentiment and engagement.
- `churn-prediction` (solo): Predict which accounts are about to slip before usage dips.
- `renewals-and-expansion` (human): Time renewal conversations and spot upsell openings; the ask is yours.
- `advocacy-and-referrals` (assisted): Turn the happiest users into referrals, reviews and case studies.
- `qbr-prep` (assisted): Build the quarterly review from real account data.

## Community
- `engagement-and-replies` (assisted): Every member question and mention gets a reply in the house voice.
- `moderation` (assisted): Spam, conduct and noise handled quietly.
- `member-spotlights` (assisted): Find and celebrate member wins.
- `event-coordination` (human): AMAs, calls and spotlights on a cadence people can count on.
