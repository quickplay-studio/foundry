# Legal

formation, documents, IP, compliance

Not in SkillTree. Added because every launch needs it. Agents draft and check; you or a solicitor sign off. Nothing here is legal advice.

14 jobs. Stage: clear.

## Formation
- `entity-and-structure` (human): Sole trader, limited company or hold off: options, costs and timing laid out; you choose.
- `tax-registration` (assisted): HMRC registrations, VAT thresholds and deadlines for the chosen structure.
- `bank-and-payments-setup` (human): Which account and payment provider fit the venture; you open them.

## Documents
- `terms-of-service` (assisted): Draft the terms for the product from the brain's offer and the market's rules.
- `privacy-policy` (assisted): Draft the privacy policy from what the product actually collects.
- `contract-drafting` (assisted): Services agreements and SLAs from a clause library.
- `nda-and-agreements` (assisted): NDAs, contractor and referral agreements from templates.

## IP
- `trademark-and-name-check` (solo): Check the venture name against UK and EU trademark registers, Companies House, domains and app stores.
- `licence-audit` (solo): Every dependency, font, image and dataset checked for a licence that allows commercial use.
- `ip-assignment` (assisted): Make sure work done by contractors or tools is owned by the venture.

## Compliance
- `platform-and-ad-compliance` (solo): App store, ad platform and UK advertising rules checked before anything is submitted or spent.
- `data-protection` (assisted): UK GDPR basics: lawful basis, data map, ICO registration need, retention.
- `consumer-law-check` (assisted): Pricing, cancellation, refunds and subscription rules the product must follow.
- `regulatory-screen` (human): Flag ideas that touch regulated ground (money, health, children, gambling) before any work starts; you decide.
