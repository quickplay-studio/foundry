# Deals

replies, calls, closing, pipeline

24 jobs. Stage: sell.

## Reply Handling
- `reply-classification` (solo): Read every reply and tag it: interested, objection, referral, not now, never.
- `objection-response` (assisted): Draft answers to common objections from a tuned library.
- `hot-lead-routing` (solo): Send interested replies straight to the calendar and the pipeline.
- `meeting-booking` (solo): Offer slots, book the call, chase no-shows.
- `referral-capture` (solo): Spot the 'talk to my colleague' replies and act on the warm intro.

## Inbound
- `speed-to-lead` (solo): Acknowledge and engage every inbound lead within minutes.
- `lead-qualification` (solo): Parse intake forms and score inbound leads before a human looks.
- `comment-cta-fulfilment` (solo): Detect keyword comments on content and deliver the promised asset by DM.
- `inbox-triage` (solo): Sort inbound email and DMs: customer, lead, partnership, noise.

## Call Cycle
- `pre-call-briefing` (solo): Brief before every call: who, company, history, objectives, talking points.
- `call-capture` (solo): Record, transcribe and file every meeting automatically.
- `post-call-debrief` (solo): Pull outcomes, action items and deal updates out of the transcript.
- `follow-up-drafting` (assisted): Draft the recap email with next steps before the prospect forgets the call.
- `objection-library` (solo): Mine every call for the objections that recur and keep the rebuttals that win.

## Deal Artifacts
- `demo-prototyping` (assisted): Turn discovery notes into a clickable prototype before the next meeting.
- `proposal-generation` (solo): Branded proposal rendered to PDF from the call context.
- `deal-room-assembly` (solo): Spin up a tracked, password-gated deal room per prospect.
- `agreement-drafting` (assisted): Produce the services agreement the moment a deal closes verbally.
- `pricing-support` (human): Model anchors, phases and ROI comparisons; the final number stays with you.

## Pipeline Ops
- `crm-hygiene` (solo): Keep records deduped, fields normalised and stages honest.
- `pipeline-reporting` (solo): Weekly pipeline state: what moved, what stalled, what closes next.
- `forecasting` (assisted): Probability-weight the pipeline into a revenue forecast.
- `reactivation` (solo): Revive dormant deals and win back lost ones on a schedule.
- `win-loss-analysis` (solo): Tag why deals close or die and feed the pattern back into targeting.
