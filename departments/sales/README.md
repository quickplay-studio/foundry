# Sales

targeting, outreach, sequencing

22 jobs. Stage: sell.

## Targeting
- `icp-definition` (solo): Write and keep the ideal customer profile per vertical: size, pains, buying triggers.
- `market-mapping` (solo): List every company in a vertical that fits the ICP before any campaign starts.
- `trigger-detection` (solo): Watch targets for hiring, funding, stack and leadership changes that mean budget.
- `lookalike-modelling` (solo): Build fresh target lists from the shape of the accounts that already bought.

## Lead Sourcing
- `database-mining` (solo): Pull company and contact lists from structured databases and open registers.
- `web-and-maps-scraping` (solo): Collect local businesses and niche directory entries as leads.
- `social-mining` (solo): Harvest engaged people from comments, followers and groups around the topic.
- `list-building` (solo): Merge, dedupe and segment raw leads into a campaign-ready list.

## Enrichment
- `contact-enrichment` (solo): Add an email, phone and LinkedIn profile to every lead.
- `email-verification` (solo): Check every address before a single send goes out.
- `account-enrichment` (solo): Attach firmographics, tech stack and headcount trend to each target account.
- `fit-scoring` (solo): Score each lead against the ICP so effort goes where it converts.

## Outreach Writing
- `personalisation-research` (solo): Build a short dossier per prospect: hooks, context, common ground.
- `cold-email-drafting` (solo): Write multi-step cold email sequences tuned to the vertical and the offer.
- `linkedin-messaging` (solo): Draft connection notes and DM sequences that read like a person wrote them.
- `proof-matching` (solo): Pick the case study or proof point that fits each prospect's industry and problem.
- `cold-call-scripting` (assisted): Call and voicemail scripts with branching objection handling.
- `video-prospecting` (assisted): Scripts for personalised prospecting videos under ninety seconds.

## Sequencing and Send
- `campaign-orchestration` (solo): Order email, LinkedIn and phone touches into one multi-channel cadence.
- `campaign-launch` (assisted): Load finished campaigns into the sending platform with the right settings.
- `deliverability` (solo): Keep sending domains healthy: warm-up, spam tests, inbox rotation.
- `send-optimisation` (solo): Time sends by timezone and pace volume to protect reply rates.
