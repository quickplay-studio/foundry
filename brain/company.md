# Company brain

The one file every agent reads before it does anything. Charlie owns it. Built and updated by `brain/INTERVIEW.md`; agents may propose edits in a pull request, never commit to it.

## Who we are
- Operator: Charlie Page-Blair, London. Solo, part time, building ventures that have to pay for themselves.
- Entity: none yet. Ventures run as personal projects until the first one makes money, then Legal `entity-and-structure` decides.
- Working hours: evenings and weekends UK time. Agents run around the clock on GitHub Actions.

## What we make
- Small software products for everyday problems (money, food, health, productivity, family, UK life), often sourced by Lodestone from what people are already asking for.
- Content channels (Quickplay Daily first: automated short-form video on four platforms) and online retail (own store, TikTok Shop, dropshipping) when the numbers in the audit hold.
- Each venture: one clear problem, one MVP, one way to charge, shipped in weeks not months.

## Who we sell to
- Default: consumers and prosumers in the UK, then English-speaking markets.
- B2B only when the audit engine says the idea has a business buyer.

## Voice
- Plain, direct, short sentences. UK English. No em or en dashes, no hype, no exclamation marks.
- Say the price and what it does in the first two lines. Proof over adjectives.

## Constraints
- Free tools only until a venture earns its first pound: free tiers, no card on file, nothing pay-as-you-go. Ad spend is the single exception and Charlie approves every budget line.
- Claude usage is budgeted: routine work on free models through GitHub, complicated work in Claude Code with Sonnet, Opus only for hard decisions.
- Nothing regulated without a human and a solicitor: finance, health, children, gambling. Legal `regulatory-screen` runs before any build.
